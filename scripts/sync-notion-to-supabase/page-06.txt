## **1. Executive Summary**
This white paper identifies ten categories of modern "necessary conveniences," quantifies their monthly costs for a single working-age adult, and outlines a realistic plan to secure them in Sydney, Australia in 2025.
- **Share-house scenario:** ≈ **A$3,210/month** (≈ A$38,500 p.a.)
- **Single-occupancy scenario:** ≈ **A$4,635/month** (≈ A$55,600 p.a.)
Housing remains by far the largest driver. All other categories combined still cost less than median one-bed rent.
---
## **2. Introduction: Concept of "Necessaries"**
Certain goods and services are considered *necessary* not only because they support basic survival but because their absence would lead to social exclusion or visible poverty. In 2025 Sydney, these necessities encompass housing, digital connectivity, transportation, and access to health services, among others.

## **3. Scope & Methodology**
- **Unit of analysis:** Single adult (25–45 yrs) in metropolitan Sydney
- **Price base:** Q2 2025 nominal Australian dollars
- **Sources:** Domain Rental Report, ABS CPI, AEMO data, NSW Government rates, etc.

## **4. The 2025 Catalogue of Necessary Conveniences**
Categories: Secure shelter, Adequate nutrition, Clothing & footwear, Utilities, Digital & telecoms, Personal & domestic goods, Health care access, Personal transport, Social participation, Contingency buffer.
---
## **5. Cost Analysis (Monthly in A$)**
Rent, Groceries, Electricity, Water/Waste, Broadband, Mobile SIM, Public Transport, Clothing & Footwear, Health Out-of-Pocket, Household & Hygiene, Social / Contingency. Total Share-House: 3,210/month. Total Solo: 4,635/month.
---
## **6. Practical Plan to Achieve the Standard**
Housing: Prioritise share-housing; explore NSW Bond Loan & Rent Choice. Utilities: Switch electricity plans yearly; claim $150 NSW energy rebate. Groceries: Shop at Aldi; use apps like WiseList; meal prep. Connectivity: $15 SIM + shared NBN. Transport: Use Opal off-peak discounts; cycle short distances. Healthcare: Use bulk-billing clinics; get a Low-Income Health Care Card. Income target: After-tax $49k (shared) / $70k (solo); gross $61k–$87k.
---
## **10. Conclusion**
A dignified life in Sydney in 2025 requires ~$38k–$56k annually, depending on living arrangement. Meeting this threshold allows people to participate in modern society with security and respect. The costliest factor remains housing.
