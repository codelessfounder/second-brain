## **Math of a $5M SAFE @ $30M Cap**
- Conversion at Series A =30M5M=16.7%
- So you're effectively selling ~17% of the company in the seed.
- That's **reasonable founder dilution** — actually cleaner than $20M cap (25%) and not as aggressive as $40M (12.5%).
---
### **Investor Perspective**
- **Pros**: $30M cap means you're valuing yourself ambitiously but not absurdly. If you have strong traction / big vision, investors will accept it. Still leaves enough ownership upside for them if Series A is at $80M–$100M+.
- **Cons**: If you're pre-product or pre-revenue, some will push back. They may ask: "What milestones will you hit with this $5M that justify Series A at $60–$100M?"
---
### **Benchmarks (Seed Market 2025)**
- **Pre-seed**: $5M–$15M caps common for idea/MVP stage.
- **Seed**: $20M–$40M caps increasingly normal if you have traction, solid team, hot market.
- **Top-tier**: $50M+ caps at seed do happen with brand-name founders or rocketship growth.
---
### **How to Frame $30M Cap**
- **Don't apologise** — position as fair and market-standard. **Point to milestones**. **Show comps**.
